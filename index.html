Growing resilience, cultivating community

Kū'ono Farm is a two-acre regenerative farm that sits on the slopes of Haleakala. We grow specialty cut flowers, tropical fruit, herbs, and seasonal produce using farming practices that build healthy soil, support biodiversity, and celebrate Hawaiʻi's agricultural heritage.

We believe a farm should be more than a place where food is grown—it should be a place where people reconnect with the land and with one another. Through educational workshops, volunteer opportunities, and seasonal gatherings for our community, we hope to cultivate a stronger, more resilient connection to agriculture.
