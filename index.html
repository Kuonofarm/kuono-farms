OUR PHILOSOPHY

We believe healthy land grows healthy communities.

Nestled in the rolling hills of Makawao, Kūʻono Farm is a regenerative farm dedicated to cultivating beautiful flowers, healthy food, and meaningful connections between people and the land.

Every season offers another opportunity to restore soil, support biodiversity, share knowledge, and create spaces where our community can gather, learn, and reconnect with agriculture.
