Resiliant ecosystem builds resiliant community.

Kū'ono Farm is a two-acre regenerative farm on the slopes of Haleakala. We grow cut flowers, tropical fruit, herbs, and seasonal produce using regenerative farming practices that build healthy soil, support biodiversity, and strengthen our local food system. Beyond growing food and flowers, we believe farms should also grow community through workshops, shared meals, and meaningful experiences that reconnect people with the land.
